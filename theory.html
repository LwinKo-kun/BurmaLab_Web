Physics Theory & Key Formulas

Essential equations and concepts for the Myanmar High School Mechanics Curriculum — Grades 10 through 12.

Full Curriculum Index →
Grade 10

Core Formulas

Kinematics & Motion (1D)

  • v = u + at
  • s = ut + (1/2)at²
  • v² = u² + 2as
  • s = ((u + v) / 2) * t

Dynamics, Forces & Friction

  • ΣF = ma (Newton's 2nd Law)
  • W = mg
  • f_s(max) = μ_s * N (Static Friction)
  • f_k = μ_k * N (Kinetic Friction)
Grade 11

Core Formulas

Work, Energy & Momentum

  • W = F * d * cos(θ)
  • KE = (1/2)mv² | PE = mgh
  • p = mv | J = Δp = F_avg * Δt
  • m1u1 + m2u2 = m1v1 + m2v2 (Conservation of Momentum)

Circular Motion & Gravitation

  • a_c = v² / r = ω²r
  • F_c = (m * v²) / r
  • v_ideal = √(r * g * tan(θ)) (Banked Turn)
  • F_g = G * (m1 * m2) / r²
Grade 12

Core Formulas

Torque, SHM & Waves

  • τ = r * F * sin(θ) (Στ = 0 for Equilibrium)
  • F_s = -kx (Hooke's Law)
  • T = 2π * √(m / k) (Spring SHM Period)
  • v = f * λ | f_n = (n * v) / (2L) (Harmonics)

Fluid Mechanics

  • P = F / A | P = P_0 + ρgh
  • F_b = ρ_fluid * V_disp * g (Archimedes' Principle)
  • A1 * v1 = A2 * v2 (Continuity Equation)
  • P + (1/2)ρv² + ρgh = constant (Bernoulli)
